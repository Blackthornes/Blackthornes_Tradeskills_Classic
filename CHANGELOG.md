# Changelog

Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org): the middle number
moves for new behaviour, the last for fixes alone. The newest version goes at
the top; a release is refused if its version has no section here.

## 0.10.1

- Various bug fixes.

## 0.10.0

- The auction house tabs now match the other Blackthornes add-ons.
- Search All now opens the buy plan for the first item.
- Item icons now have a ring and a name colour that match their quality.
- Stack counts show on the corner of item icons.
- Items show a small mark and colour for what to do with them.
- The sell list shows each price in gold, silver and copper coins.
- Various bug fixes.

## 0.9.0

- All your Blackthornes add-ons now open in one window, with a button for each add-on down the side.
- The window moves beside the auction house, mailbox, vendor and trainer when you open them.
- The auction house now shows the price of the item you searched for.
- Various bug fixes.

## 0.8.0

- Every window has a new look that matches the other Blackthornes add-ons.
- The Disenchanting tab now has a Buy button for gear deals.
- Various bug fixes.

## 0.7.0

- Enchanters now see disenchant results and chances on item tooltips.
- The minimap button has a new Blackthornes badge.

- Various bug fixes.

## 0.6.0

## 0.1.0

First release for WoW Classic Era.

- **Level up:** the cheapest craft for your next skill point, and Road to 300 with every step and what it costs.
- **Learn Next:** the recipes to learn next and where the trainer or vendor is, with an arrow.
- **Crafting for gold:** what each craft costs and sells for, a craft queue that adds up your materials, and a shopping list.
- **Craft button:** crafts from the queue with one click.
- **Auction house:** scan prices at the auction house.
- **All your characters:** recipes, gold and professions in one place, and profession cooldowns with a note in chat when one is ready.
- **Languages:** English, Deutsch (German), French, Spanish, Russian and Simplified Chinese.
