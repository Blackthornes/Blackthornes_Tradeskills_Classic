# Changelog

Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org): the middle number
moves for new behaviour, the last for fixes alone. The newest version goes at
the top; a release is refused if its version has no section here.

## 0.4.0

- The Road to 300 and Learn Next now send you to the closest trainer.
- The Road to 300 lets you pick recipes by lowest cost or real cost.
- Various bug fixes.

## 0.3.1

- Various bug fixes.

## 0.3.0

- The auction house List button now lists in one press.
- The auction house Buy button buys your queue's materials stack by stack.
- Item tooltips show what a vendor sells the item for.
- Various bug fixes.

## 0.2.1

- Various bug fixes.

## 0.2.0

- The Road to 300 now tells you when you need to buy a recipe.
- Various bug fixes.

## 0.1.2

- **Fixes:** Listing on Era's auction house no longer stops with an internal auction error. The next stack waits in the auction slot, so each click lists one.

## 0.1.1

- **Fixes:** Listing items on Era's auction house now works. List Next checks prices once up front, keeps copper prices, and posts each item with the game's own call.

## 0.1.0

First release for WoW Classic Era.

- **Level up:** the cheapest craft for your next skill point, and Road to 300 with every step and what it costs.
- **Learn Next:** the recipes to learn next and where the trainer or vendor is, with an arrow.
- **Crafting for gold:** what each craft costs and sells for, a craft queue that adds up your materials, and a shopping list.
- **Craft button:** crafts from the queue with one click.
- **Auction house:** scan prices at the auction house.
- **All your characters:** recipes, gold and professions in one place, and profession cooldowns with a note in chat when one is ready.
- **Languages:** English, Deutsch (German), French, Spanish, Russian and Simplified Chinese.
